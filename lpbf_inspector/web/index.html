How does this analysis work?

Calibration measures the average gray level of the first 20 images in this job, separately for each sequence when the interval is 2. These initial images are not assumed to be defect-free.

Each area is compared with its recent history within this build. The analysis tracks intensity, texture, local drift and overall brightness. The adjustable threshold sets the minimum change to flag; variation in the recent history can increase this threshold. Researcher annotations and other jobs are not used.

For Aalto, an interval of 2 separates even and odd acquisitions without assigning melting or spreading stages. Normal geometry changes can also trigger indications. A stable anomaly present from the start can remain invisible to temporal analysis.

1 = show all. Indications must share a category, camera and stage, with overlapping areas in successive images. A gap breaks the sequence. This filter applies to the list, 3D view and report without rerunning the analysis or deleting decisions.

How is this score calculated?

The score gives equal weight to change intensity and persistence: 100 × √(V × P). V = r / (r + 2), where r is the peak detection-threshold ratio; P = n / (n + 3), where n is the number of consecutive comparable images. A stronger or more persistent change increases priority.

This score helps prioritize review. It depends on the job settings and does not represent defect probability or material severity.

Public image libraries and provenance

Two additional jobs contain 200 photographs each: 100 consecutive layers, after spreading and after melting.

  • NIST · Three-Dimensional Scan Strategies : layers 2–101 at 20 µm. Original LayerCamera photographs converted losslessly to PNG, without annotations.
  • ORNL · 64 cylinders in 316H (2025) : layers 70–169 at 100 µm. Visible-light channels extracted without annotations from the public HDF5 dataset mirror. The data producers have already corrected lighting and perspective.

Each library represents one job. These extracts occupy approximately 1.26 GB; complete archives are not installed. Use Import folder to add your own JPEG or PNG build images. Generic HDF5 import is not yet available.
